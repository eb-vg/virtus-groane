Welcome to Virtus Groane ASD

Track & Field, Triathlon, Nordic Walking & Fitness in the North Milan Area (Italy)

About Virtus Groane ASD

Founded in 1978 and officially renamed Virtus Groane ASD in 2021, our amateur sports association (non-profit ASD) is affiliated with the Italian Athletics Federation (FIDAL), the Italian Triathlon Federation (FITRI), and AICS. We operate in the North Milan area across the municipalities of Senago, Solaro, and Limbiate.

Our Mission & Key Programs

Youth Athletics Academy

Fostering healthy development, teamwork, and athletic skills (running, jumping, throwing) in a supportive educational environment for children and teenagers.

Competitive & Master Sectors

Supporting athletes from junior categories to Master runners participating in national and international marathons, trail runs, and multisport triathlon events across Europe.

Community & Wellness

Offering inclusive fitness programs such as Nordic Walking in nature trails, Running School for adult road runners, and women's fitness ("Mamme che Sport!").

International Projects & Partnerships

Virtus Groane ASD is open to international collaborations, sports exchanges, and cross-border partnership initiatives with sports clubs, schools, and athletic associations across Europe and worldwide.
