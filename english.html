Welcome to Virtus Groane

Virtus Groane is a non-profit Amateur Sports Association (ASD) affiliated with the Italian Athletics Federation (FIDAL) and the Italian Triathlon Federation (FITRI). We operate in the towns of Senago, Solaro, and Limbiate (near Milan, Italy).

We offer courses and training sessions for athletes of all ages and levels:

  • Youth Athletics (Track & Field): From 5 years old up to Under-20, focusing on motor skills and track disciplines.
  • Running School: Running for fitness or competition with our certified coaches.
  • Triathlon: Swimming, cycling, and running preparation for Juniores and Masters.
  • Nordic Walking: Health and fitness walks in the Groane Park using poles.

For more information on schedules, fees, or to request a free trial, please contact us:

  • Email: info@virtusgroane.com
